# Looking after your mind in a crisis (English)

Oracle OS guide (WHO Psychological First Aid). Fear, anxiety, sadness and irritability are **normal** reactions to abnormal events.

## For yourself
Slow breathing (in for 4, out for 6, five times); 5-4-3-2-1 grounding (see, hear, touch, smell, taste); keep routines; limit news to set times; do one useful task a day; sleep, drink water, eat, move; avoid alcohol and drugs; talk to someone you trust.

## Helping others — LOOK, LISTEN, LINK
Look: safety first, who needs urgent help. Listen: approach calmly, ask about needs, don't pressure people to tell their story, don't judge or make false promises. Link: water, food, shelter, information, family contact, services. Children: keep them with carers, explain simply and truthfully, let them play, keep routines.

## Warning signs — get help now
Thoughts of death or self-harm, unable to care for oneself for days, severe confusion, hearing/seeing things others don't, uncontrolled alcohol or drug use. Emergency **112 / 999 / 911**; UK & Ireland **Samaritans 116 123**; USA & Canada **988**; Portugal **SNS 24 808 24 24 24**; Brazil **CVV 188**.

Oracle's chat companion can listen and help you calm down but is **not a psychologist or doctor**.
