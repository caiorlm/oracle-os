# The 50-year plan — surviving and rebuilding after a total collapse

Oracle OS guide. It is the "map" linking every other guide and manual in the library. Think of it in phases: priorities change in each. A phone won't last 50 years — Oracle's job is to **pass the knowledge on to people and paper** while it works.

## Rules for every phase
1. **People first**: nobody lasts decades alone. Join trusted neighbours, share tasks, care for the most vulnerable.
2. **Water, shelter, fire, food, health** — in that order, almost always.
3. **Record**: write down on paper what works (seeds, dates, recipes, illnesses, births). Collective memory is the new "hard drive".
4. **Teach**: every skill should be known by at least 3 people.
5. **Oracle's power**: charge with a solar panel at midday; use power-saving mode; copy the library to another device and keep it switched off in a metal (Faraday) bag.

## Phase 0 — first 72 hours
- Safety and the injured: "First aid", "Search and rescue".
- Water: store all the water you can now (baths, jerrycans, hot-water cylinder). See "Drinking water".
- Shelter and warmth: "Blackout, cold and shelter"; never braziers indoors.
- Information: FM/AM radio (Radio tab), coordinate with neighbours, meeting point.
- Inventory: food, medicines, tools, fuel, people and what each one can do.

## Phase 1 — first month
- Secure water: source, filter, disinfection (boil, chlorine, SODIS), latrines 30+ m from wells and rivers.
- Food: ration stores, preserve perishables (salt, dry, smoke — "Food preservation"), fish, gather known plants (use "Identify a plant" but always confirm).
- Hygiene: wash hands with soap/ash, keep waste and faeces away from water — prevents cholera and dysentery (the big killers after disasters).
- Health: kit, essential medicines, mental health ("Mental health in a crisis", meditation, routine).
- Organisation: short daily meeting, watch rota, record of who needs what ("Community and organisation").

## Phase 2 — first year
- **Sow**: choose open-pollinated seeds, prepare soil, compost, sowing calendar ("Seeds and grains", gardening books in the library). Save seed from the best plant of each harvest.
- **Animals**: chickens and rabbits (fast to breed), goats for milk ("Animals and veterinary care").
- **Store for winter**: dry, salt, smoke, cool cellar, preserves.
- **Durable shelter**: insulate, efficient (rocket) stove, chimney, watertight roof ("Natural building").
- **Long-term water**: protected hand-dug well, rainwater harvesting with a sand filter.
- **Health**: midwifery and newborn care ("Midwifery and medicinal plants"), teeth, wounds, isolating the sick.
- **Tools**: sharpening, handle repair, charcoal, first smithing from scrap.

## Phase 3 — years 2 to 5
- Crop rotation (grains, legumes, roots, fallow), orchards and grafting, beekeeping (honey, wax, pollination).
- **Crafts**: smithing, pottery and kilns, carpentry, soap and candles ("Basic chemistry"), textiles (flax and wool: card, spin, weave), tanning hides and making shoes.
- **Off-grid energy**: water wheel/mill for grinding, dynamo to charge Oracle, animal traction ("Off-grid energy").
- **School**: teach reading, writing, counting, measuring, first aid and farming — every day ("Community and education").
- **Communication**: signals, Morse with light and mirror, messengers, hand-drawn maps ("Communication without power").
- **Rules and justice**: written agreements, conflict mediation, fair sharing.

## Phase 4 — years 5 to 20
- Surpluses and trade with other communities: seeds, tools, cloth, salt, knowledge.
- Common weights, measures and calendar; records of property and births.
- Specialisation: smith, potter, midwife, teacher, healer, builder — with apprentices.
- Copy essential books by hand or print while paper lasts; create a community library.
- Recover simple technology: mills, lime kilns, bricks, crude glass, paper.

## Phase 5 — years 20 to 50
- The generation born after the collapse takes over: it must have learned all this in practice.
- Writing, maths and basic science make it possible to rebuild the rest: chemistry, metallurgy, mechanics, medicine (LibreTexts, OpenStax and Wikipedia in the library are the "manual for rebuilding civilisation").
- Public health: clean water, vaccines if any exist, hygiene, nutrition — they triple life expectancy.

## What to print now (while there is power)
First aid; drinking water; food preservation; seeds and grains; midwifery; fire and shelter; navigation; wild plants (dangerous look-alikes); soap; this plan. Use Oracle's print button ("Print pack") and keep it in a waterproof bag.

## What to buy/gather physically
USB solar panel 20–60 W + LiFePO4 power bank; spare phone with an Oracle copy in a Faraday bag; open-pollinated seeds; hand tools (axe, saw, spade, hoe, hammer, files, sharpening stone, needles, thread); cast-iron pots; medical kit; water filters; lighters and ferro rod; tarps; rope; paper and pencils.
