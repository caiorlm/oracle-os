# Animals — raising, feeding, treating and using them

Oracle OS guide. **Illustrated** manuals in the library: CVM "Raising Healthy" (goats, rabbits, poultry, pigs, sheep, cattle) and "Drugs and their usage", Peace Corps and FAO (beekeeping), Langstroth, "Poultry", "Sheep, Swine, and Poultry", "Pratt's Practical Pointers", "Domestic Animals"; cheese and bread in "Seeds and grains".

## Where to start
1. **Chickens**: daily eggs, meat, eat scraps and insects, excellent manure. Coop shut at night (foxes), 4–5 m² of run per bird, nest box, perch, clean water. A broody hen sits ~21 days; keep a cockerel for chicks.
2. **Rabbits**: fast breeding (gestation ~31 days, 6–8 kits), eat grass and garden scraps, lean meat. Dry shaded hutches; keep bucks apart.
3. **Goats**: milk (1–3 L/day), meat, hide; browse scrub. Strong fences; goats need company. Gestation ~150 days.
4. **Bees**: honey (sugar and antiseptic), wax (candles, waterproofing), pollination. See "Small-scale beekeeping".
5. **Pigs, sheep and cattle**: need more land and feed; sheep give wool (see "Textiles and leather"), cattle milk and traction.

## Animal health — signs of illness
Isolate any animal that: stops eating, is dull, has diarrhoea, cough, nasal/eye discharge, lameness, weight loss or fever. Wash hands before and after. New animals stay in quarantine 2–3 weeks before joining the group.
- **Parasites**: rotate pastures, clean bedding, check the inner eyelid colour (pale = worm anaemia; see the FAMACHA method in the manuals).
- **Wounds**: clean, remove maggots, disinfect, protect from flies.
- **Birth**: mostly unaided; intervene only if straining >30–60 min without progress — clean, lubricated hands, pull with contractions, down and back.
- **Zoonoses** (spread to people): rabies (bites — wash 15 min), brucellosis (boil milk!), tuberculosis, salmonella (wash dirty eggs, cook meat well), mange, ringworm.

## Feeding
Pasture and hay (cut and dry grass in summer for winter), garden scraps, cracked grain, acorns (pigs), dried nettles (hens). Clean water always. Salt/minerals for ruminants.

## Using the products
- **Milk**: milk with clean hands and udder; **boil or pasteurise** (72 °C for 15 s or 63 °C for 30 min). Cheese: rennet (kid/calf stomach) or acid (lemon, vinegar); see "The Book of Cheese".
- **Slaughter and meat**: stun first (firm blow to the head), bleed at once, hang, skin/pluck, gut without tearing intestines, cool. Preserve: salt, smoke, dry, lard.
- **Hides and wool**: see "Textiles and leather". **Bones**: tools, needles, glue (boil). **Fat**: candles, soap ("Basic chemistry"). **Manure**: compost and, dried, fuel.

## Animal traction
Donkeys, horses and oxen: well-fitted yoke/collar, trimmed hooves, rest and water every 1–2 h of work. Plough, cart, water wheel (noria), millstone.
