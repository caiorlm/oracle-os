# Emergency drinking water — find, treat, store (English)

Oracle OS guide based on CDC, WHO, FEMA and IFRC guidance (see library: IFRC Household Water Treatment, WHO/WEDC notes, Practical Action SODIS).

## How much
- At least **4 litres (1 gallon) per person per day** for drinking and cooking; more in heat, illness, pregnancy or hard work.
- Store at least **3 days** (72 hours); ideally 1–2 weeks. Remember pets.

## Water already in your home
- Stored bottles; the **hot-water tank** (turn off power/gas, close the inlet, open the drain valve); the **toilet cistern** (tank, not bowl, and no coloured cleaners) — treat it; ice cubes; liquid in canned fruit and vegetables.
- House pipes: open the highest tap to let air in, collect from the lowest.
- **Never** drink untreated radiator, pool, central heating or flood water.

## Treat it
1. **Settle and strain** cloudy water through a clean cloth or coffee filter (removes dirt only).
2. **Boil**: rolling boil for **1 minute** (3 minutes above 2,000 m). Most reliable against germs.
3. **Unscented household bleach** (5–6%): **2 drops per litre** (8 drops per gallon), stir, wait **30 minutes**. It should smell faintly of chlorine; if not, repeat and wait 15 more minutes. Double for cloudy or very cold water. Never use scented, gel or detergent bleach.
4. **Purification tablets**: follow the pack.
5. **SODIS**: clear PET bottles up to 2 L, clear water, direct sun for **6 hours** (2 days if cloudy).
6. **Portable filters** remove bacteria and protozoa; many do not remove viruses — add chlorine or boil if possible.

None of these remove fuel, chemicals or heavy metals. If water smells of fuel or looks oily or foamy, find another source.

## Store it
Food-grade, clean, sealed containers away from sunlight and chemicals. Sanitise with 1 teaspoon of bleach per litre, rinse. Replace home-stored water every 6 months. Pour out — don't dip hands or cups in.

## Rainwater
Clean roof and gutters, discard the first flush, cover the container, always treat before drinking.

## Dehydration
Thirst, dark scanty urine, dizziness, dry mouth. Children: no tears, dry nappy. Give small frequent sips; for diarrhoea use oral rehydration salts (WHO home recipe if none: 1 litre safe water + 6 level teaspoons sugar + ½ level teaspoon salt).
