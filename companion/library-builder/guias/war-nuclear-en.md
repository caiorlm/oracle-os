# War, attacks, nuclear explosion and chemical incidents (English)

Oracle OS guide (FEMA, CDC, Sweden's "In case of crisis or war", Norway/Finland guides in the library). Always follow official instructions on radio and alerts.

## GO IN, STAY IN, TUNE IN
Get into the nearest solid (brick/concrete) building, stay inside away from windows, tune to public radio (UK: BBC Radio 4 / BBC Radio Scotland — see Radio screen).

## Sirens and alerts
Meanings vary by country. On hearing one: go inside, close doors/windows, turn on the radio. Keep phone emergency alerts enabled.

## Air raid / shelling
Public shelter, basement, underground car park or metro; at home the innermost windowless room on the lowest floor. Put two walls between you and outside. Stay away from glass. Do not film troops or strike sites; do not post locations online.

## Nuclear explosion
- **Don't look at the flash.** Outdoors: lie down, cover your head.
- You have **minutes** before fallout arrives: **get inside** the best shelter — centre of a concrete building or basement.
- **Stay inside at least 24 hours** unless told otherwise; fallout radiation drops sharply over the first hours and days.
- If exposed: **remove outer clothing** before entering (removes up to ~90% of contamination), bag it away from people, shower with soap and shampoo, **no conditioner**; blow your nose, wipe ears and eyelids.
- Sealed food and water are safe — wipe the packaging first.
- **Potassium iodide (KI)** protects only the thyroid and should be taken **only when health officials say so**.

## Chemical release
Go inside, close windows, turn off ventilation, seal gaps with wet towels and tape; many toxic gases are heavier than air — go **upstairs**. Outdoors: move crosswind and upwind. Skin contact: remove clothing, wash with lots of soap and water, flush eyes 15 minutes.

## Evacuation
72-hour grab bag: water, food, medicines and prescriptions, ID/documents (copies on phone too), cash, charger and power bank, battery radio, torch, warm clothes, first-aid kit, paper list of contacts. Agree a meeting point near home and one outside town. Take pets if you can.

## Checkpoints and afterwards
Carry ID; obey checkpoints; show your hands; don't run. Don't return home until allowed. Beware damaged buildings, gas leaks (no flames or switches), fallen power lines and unexploded ordnance — **never touch military objects**; move away and report.
